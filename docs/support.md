## Frequently asked questions

### How do I connect my Google Calendar?
Open CalWidget and click **Sign in with Google**. Google asks you to allow read-only access to your calendar. You stay signed in until you sign out or remove access in your Google account.

### Google says the app isn't verified
Click **Advanced**, then **Go to CalWidget (unsafe)**. Google shows this warning for apps that haven't finished its verification process. The app only asks to read your calendar.

### I have to sign in again every week
While an app is still in Google's testing phase, Google expires sign-in tokens after 7 days. That ends once the app is published and verified. If it keeps happening, email or open an issue and tell us which app version you're using.

### The widget shows old or no events
- Open the app to confirm that you're signed in.
- Widgets refresh on a schedule that macOS controls, so changes in Google Calendar can take a few minutes to appear.
- Remove the widget and add it again.

### The widget doesn't appear in the widget gallery
Open the app once, then check Edit Widgets again. If it's still missing, log out and back in, or restart your Mac.

### Which calendars are shown?
The calendars you've selected in the app, unless you pick different ones for a particular widget. Right-click the widget, choose **Edit Widget**, and choose **Calendars**. The widget uses the colors you've set in Google Calendar.

### How do I change a widget's style?
Right-click the widget and choose **Edit Widget**, then pick a **Style**: Custom (your colors from the app), Dark, Light, or Automatic, which follows macOS Light and Dark. Custom colors, event colors, the first day of the week and the + button are set in the app's **Appearance** pane.

### How do I get the glass (see-through) look?
That look comes from macOS, not from the app. Open **System Settings, Desktop & Dock**, and set **Widget style** to **Clear** (or Tinted). macOS then replaces the widget's background with Liquid Glass and adapts its colors.

### What do the arrows and the + button do?
The arrows move the widget to the previous or next month, and it returns to the current month after a few minutes. The **+** opens Google Calendar's new-event page in your browser. CalWidget comes to the front for a moment first, because macOS passes widget links through the app.

### Can the app change my events?
No. It asks for read-only access.

### How do I sign out or remove access?
Choose **Sign out** in the app. To also revoke access on Google's side, go to [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
